# In-game tests

What the party still needs to try in game, and which advice depends on a bug.
This isn't shown on the site. When a result comes in, update the note it
affects and remove the test from this list.

The wiki was last checked against these notes on 2026-11-04.

## Tests

### Vesper

- **Helmet of Arcane Acuity triggers.** At level 5 or higher, hit once with
  Booming Blade and read the Arcane Acuity turns. 4 turns means two triggers.
  2 turns means it only ties the Hat of Storm Scion's Power.
- **Cloak of Cunning Brume outside combat.** With nobody around, Disengage,
  then switch to turn-based mode. Whether the theft step works, or she needs a
  Darkness arrow.
- **Hiding next to a target.** Hide in fog or Darkness within melee range of
  someone. The wiki says both that this auto-succeeds and that melee range
  spots her.
- **Shield of Thralls with Vivacious Cloak.** With the shield up, cast a spell
  in melee in the cloak. Whether the cloak replaces the shield with no burst,
  or does nothing.
- **Shield of Thralls stun timing.** Break the shield on her turn, then on an
  enemy's turn. Whether the stun ever lasts until her next attack.
- **Psionic Overload with Arcane Acuity.** With both active, watch the turn
  count at the start of her turn. Whether Overload strips 2 turns each turn.
- **Psionic Overload with Lightning Charges.** With charges up, watch the
  self-damage in the combat log. How much it really costs her each turn.
- **Uncanny Dodge and reactions.** With Overload on, watch her reaction icon.
  Also dodge an enemy, then back away from it. Whether a self-hit spends her
  reaction, and whether dodged enemies lose their opportunity attack.
- **Savage Attacker on Booming Blade.** Read the double rolls in the combat
  log. Whether the thunder dice roll twice as well.
- **Sneak Attack and her reaction.** Land a Sneak Attack and watch her
  reaction icon. Whether it leaves her reaction free.
- **Friends after a failed pickpocket.** Fail a pickpocket on purpose after
  saving. Whether there's any chance to cast it.
- **A different disguise.** Get caught, change disguise, and try the same
  target. Whether the victim recognises her.
- **Mage Hand and lights.** Have the Hand try to turn off a light. Whether the
  lights-out tip works.
- **Declining the Emperor's tadpole.** Save first, then have a party member
  who never used a parasite lead. Who rolls in co-op, and what failing does.

- **The Reviving Hands uptime.** Wearing the Helm of Balduran, read Blade
  Ward's turns at the start of her second turn in a fight, and whether it's
  still up on enemy turns that round. If it only covers every other round,
  weigh the gloves against Braindrain again. A web search on 2026-11-04 found
  no report either way.

- **Enhance Leap range and unpreparing.** Cast it out of combat and check the
  jump range (the wiki's formula gives about 13.5 m at STR 8). Then unprepare
  it and see whether the buff stays, as that decides if it needs a permanent
  spot. Do the same for Longstrider.
- **Scribed Bone Chill.** Scribe the scroll at level 6 and see whether the
  cantrip needs a prepare slot, or is always available like her others.

### Alene

- **Boots of Stormy Clamour with Radiating Orb.** Hit with radiant damage in
  Luminous gear, then with Coruscation Ring. Which sources add Reverberation.

### Pearl

- **Shield bonuses with a bow drawn.** Compare AC and initiative with the bow
  drawn. Whether Sentinel Shield's and Rhapsody's bonuses count.
- **Rhapsody stacks.** Kill something with the bow and check for stacks and
  arrow damage. Whether it's worth the shield.
- **Ring of Regeneration with Broodmother's Revenge.** In Act 3, start a fight
  and read her bow's conditions at the start of her first turn. Whether the
  poison coating is up before her first shot, and stays up every turn.
- **When Mol pays for the idol.** Give her the idol and see whether the Ring of
  Protection comes straight away or only when you find her in Baldur's Gate.

### Zetharis

- **Hexblade's Curse on Eldritch Blast.** Curse a target and read the beam
  damage. Whether it belongs in the turn.
- **Gontr Mael's Haste.** Try casting it with the bow in the ranged slot, not
  drawn. Whether Zetharis has to draw it to get Haste.
- **Armour of Agathys with temporary HP.** Cast it, then gain temporary HP
  from The Lifebringer or Potent Robe. Which one wins.
- **Reverberation at 5 turns.** Fire three beams with Spineshudder and Ring of
  Spiteful Thunder. Whether the third beam can still Daze.
